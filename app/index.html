使用說明

一、開始之前：準備輝達金鑰

千枝物語用 AI 寫故事、寫英文闖關草稿，需要你自己的輝達 API Key（免費申請）。示範故事不需要金鑰。

  1. 到 build.nvidia.com 登入，產生一把新的 API Key，複製 nvapi- 開頭的整串文字。
  2. 回到標題畫面按「設定」，把金鑰貼到「輝達 API Key」欄位，按「儲存並測試」。看到測試成功就可以用了。
  3. 網頁版的「轉送網址」已經預先填好，一般不用改。

金鑰只存在這台電腦的瀏覽器裡，不會上傳到本網站。用公用電腦時，用完請到設定清空金鑰。

二、SDGs 英文闖關：做一個五關的英文互動故事

這個模式對應英文課的學習單：每個遊戲有遊戲名稱、主要角色、英文故事背景，共五關。每關有英文對話和一題 A／B 選擇題，只有一個正確答案；答對才能進下一關，答錯會出現英文提示並重新作答。五關都過了，播放結局，最後出現「Mission Complete! 任務完成！」。

從標題畫面按「SDGs 英文闖關」進入。

三、製作步驟

  1. 按「＋ 製作新的闖關遊戲」。
  2. 第一步：基本設定。填遊戲名稱，選一個 SDG 主題：SDG 6 乾淨水資源、SDG 11 永續城市與社區、SDG 12 減少浪費、SDG 13 氣候行動，或「其他」（自己填主題）。 新增主要角色（可以超過兩位，最多六位），每位寫英文名字和一句介紹。再寫地點、問題、目標，這些可以用中文寫。
  3. 讓 AI 寫英文草稿（選用）。按「AI 幫我寫英文草稿」，大約 1 到 3 分鐘，AI 會依你的設定寫出英文故事背景、五關對話與題目、提示和結局，用簡單英文（約國高中程度）。正確答案會隨機放在 A 或 B。
  4. 第二步：修改成自己的作品。每個欄位都可以直接改。每關要有：關卡名稱、至少一句對話（說話者和英文句子）、題目、選項 A、選項 B、勾選哪一個是正確答案、答錯時顯示的英文提示。
  5. 結局：寫幾句英文結局對話。「Mission Complete! 任務完成！」會自動加在最後，不用自己打。
  6. 按「儲存並試玩」。如果還有沒填的欄位，下方會列出缺哪一關、缺什麼。

不想用 AI 也可以：跳過草稿按鈕，從第二步自己寫。每關的行動都要和你選的 SDG 有關，例如 SDG 6 就是撿垃圾讓河水變乾淨、節約用水、修漏水的水管。

四、好題目的寫法

  • 兩個選項都要「有人可能會選」，但只有一個對 SDG 比較好。例如「A. Throw the bottles into the river.／B. Put the bottles in the recycling bin.」。
  • 提示不要直接說答案，要提醒想法，例如「Hint: Think about what is better for the fish in the river.」。
  • 五關要接成一個故事，一關比一關更接近目標。
  • 下方「答案」一行會顯示五關的正確答案（例如 1B 2A 3A 4B 5A），方便核對。

五、遊玩方式

  • 先看故事背景與角色，按「開始闖關」。
  • 對話一次出現一句，按「下一句」或點對話區繼續。
  • 對話播完出現題目，按 A 或 B（鍵盤也可以按 A、B 鍵）。答錯會出現紅色的 Hint，再選一次；答對出現 Correct!，按「下一關」。
  • 右上角「🔊 朗讀」會用英文唸出目前的句子、題目或提示，可以練聽力和發音。
  • 過完五關播放結局，最後顯示「Mission Complete! 任務完成！」和星星：全對 3 顆，錯 1 到 2 次 2 顆，錯更多次 1 顆。

六、交作業與分享

  • 下載：在闖關列表按「下載」，得到一個 .sdgquest.json 檔，可以交給老師，或傳給同學。
  • 匯入：在闖關列表按「匯入闖關檔」，選擇別人給你的 .sdgquest.json，就能玩對方的遊戲。
  • 複製學習單格式：在編輯畫面按「複製學習單格式」，整份內容會依學習單的順序（Game Title、Main Characters、Story Background、Level 1 到 5、Ending、Answer Key）複製起來，可以貼到 Word 或 Google 文件交出。
  • 闖關遊戲存在這台電腦的瀏覽器裡。換電腦或清除瀏覽器資料前，請先下載檔案保存。

七、故事模式（原本的玩法）

  • 新的故事：選預設世界或自己寫世界與角色，AI 會一邊畫圖一邊寫故事，每一輪給你幾個選項，也可以自己輸入行動。
  • 存檔、讀檔、節點樹：遊戲中右上角可以存檔，節點樹可以回到任何一輪改走別條路。
  • 下載進度到電腦：把故事與圖片打包下載，換電腦時用「讀取資料夾進度」載入。

八、遇到問題

  • AI 很忙或等很久：輝達免費額度高峰時會排隊，畫面會顯示等待進度，可以按「立即重試」或「取消」稍後再試。
  • 顯示金鑰錯誤：回設定重新貼上金鑰並測試。
  • 更多錯誤訊息的說明：開啟常見問題。

1. Before you start: an NVIDIA key

The game uses AI to write stories and English quest drafts, so it needs your own NVIDIA API Key (free). The demo story needs no key.

  1. Sign in at build.nvidia.com, create a new API Key and copy the whole text that starts with nvapi-.
  2. On the title screen press Settings, paste the key into "NVIDIA API Key" and press "Save and test".
  3. The relay URL of the web version is already filled in; you usually do not need to change it.

The key is kept only in this computer's browser. On a shared computer, clear it in Settings when you are done.

2. SDGs English Quest: a five-level English story game

This mode follows the English class worksheet: each game has a title, main characters and an English story background, and five levels. Each level has English dialogue and one A/B question with exactly one correct answer. A correct answer opens the next level; a wrong answer shows an English hint and the player tries again. After level 5 the ending plays, then "Mission Complete! 任務完成！".

Press "SDGs English Quest" on the title screen.

3. Making a quest

  1. Press "+ Make a new quest".
  2. Step 1: basic info. Type the game title and pick an SDG: SDG 6 Clean Water, SDG 11 Sustainable Communities, SDG 12 Reduce Waste, SDG 13 Climate Action, or Other (type your own). Add the main characters (more than two is fine, up to six) with an English name and a short description, then the place, the problem and the goal.
  3. Let the AI write an English draft (optional). Press "AI: write an English draft". In about 1 to 3 minutes it writes the background, five levels of dialogue and questions, hints and the ending in simple English. The correct answer is put on A or B at random.
  4. Step 2: make it your own. Edit any field. Every level needs a title, at least one dialogue line (speaker and English text), a question, option A, option B, which option is correct, and a hint for a wrong answer.
  5. Ending: a few English lines. "Mission Complete! 任務完成！" is added at the end for you.
  6. Press "Save and test". Anything still missing is listed at the bottom.

You can skip the AI and write everything yourself. Each level's action should fit your SDG, for example cleaning a river or saving water for SDG 6.

4. Writing good questions

  • Both options should be something a person might choose, but only one is better for the SDG.
  • A hint helps the player think again; it does not just give the answer.
  • The five levels make one story that moves toward the goal.
  • The "Answers" line at the bottom shows the key, for example 1B 2A 3A 4B 5A.

5. Playing

  • Read the background and the characters, then press "Start".
  • Dialogue appears one line at a time: press "Next" or click the dialogue.
  • Answer with A or B (the A and B keys work too). A wrong answer shows a hint; try again. A correct answer shows Correct! and "Next level".
  • "🔊 Read aloud" reads the current line, question or hint in English.
  • The end shows "Mission Complete! 任務完成！" and stars: 3 with no mistakes, 2 with one or two, 1 with more.

6. Handing in and sharing

  • Download: in the quest list press "Download" to get a .sdgquest.json file for your teacher or classmates.
  • Import: press "Import quest file" and pick a .sdgquest.json to play someone else's quest.
  • Copy as worksheet: in the editor, copies the whole quest in worksheet order (Game Title, Main Characters, Story Background, Levels 1 to 5, Ending, Answer Key) to paste into a document.
  • Quests are kept in this computer's browser. Download them before you change computers or clear browser data.

7. Story mode

  • New Story: pick a preset world or write your own; the AI writes and draws the story and offers choices each turn, or you type your own action.
  • Save, Load and the node tree: save from the toolbar; the node tree takes you back to any turn to try another path.
  • Download Progress: saves stories and pictures to your computer; "Load Progress Folder" brings them back.

8. Problems

  • The AI is busy or slow: NVIDIA's free service queues at busy times. The wait is shown; press "Retry now" or "Cancel" and try later.
  • A key error: paste the key again in Settings and test it.
  • More about error messages: open the FAQ.
